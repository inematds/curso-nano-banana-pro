A campaign with identity

From reference to review: build the fictional Brisa campaign with a product, character, text, and visual sequence. Complete it in small steps.

start now →

6 modules · 18 lessons of ~15 minutes · read on your phone or computer

What you will be able to do

Write clear promptsTurn your intent into decisions about light, shape, and composition.
Revise without starting overCompare attempts and use visible criteria.
Deliver a setOrganize product photos, scenes, a poster, infographic, and sequence.

How to study

Read a lesson and set aside ten minutes for the activity. Your progress stays in this browser. Export your journey to make a copy.

The images are illustrations generated in Codex. Image generation activities require access to an image service and may use credits. The course guides you through Gemini and asks you to record the model actually used. Codex illustrations are not Nano Banana results.

Who it is for

It is for you if you

  • Create images for a store or craft business and want to show products clearly.
  • You already use an image chat and have little time per session.
  • Prefer to learn by doing.

It is not for you if you

  • Already know the subject well and want technical material.
  • Want a traditional video lesson.
